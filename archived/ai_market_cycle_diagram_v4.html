Global spillover

How the same cycle is landing outside the US — Europe, China, Japan, South Korea.

Europe

macro2026EU lags badly in AI investment and compute; Mistral (its leading model effort) barely registers against US/China frontier labs.
macro2026New EU compute package should lift European AI investment somewhat, but won't close the structural gap to the US.
macro2026 outlookING: 2026 could mark "peak of inflated expectations" for AI in Europe as a Gartner-style hype cycle plays out.

China

micro2026Huawei AI chip revenue to hit ~$12B (+60% YoY), Ascend output scaling to ~1.6M dies as China builds a parallel hardware/software stack.
macroJan 2026US shifts Nvidia H200 export policy to case-by-case with a 25% tariff; Chinese orders could reach $14B in 2026.
macroMid-2026China's domestic chips remain an estimated 3-5 years behind the US-Taiwan frontier; CUDA lock-in still slows the switch to Huawei's stack.
macroMar 2026China's 15th Five-Year Plan embeds AI as a national priority on par with defense.

Japan

microFeb–Apr 2026Government pushes Rapidus funding to ¥2.6T ($16.3B) total, chasing 2nm production by 2027 — bigger than Germany + France chip subsidies combined.
microApr 13, 2026SoftBank + Sony + Honda + NEC launch a $1.3B domestic foundation-model consortium, anchored by Honda shipping it in vehicles by 2028.
macroJan/Feb/Jun 2026On the receiving end of China's 3-round export bans — rare-earth and dual-use squeeze hits Japanese chip and defense suppliers directly.
macro2026Rapidus has yet to ship a commercial chip; still chasing a moving target (TSMC/Samsung already at 2nm volume).

South Korea

macroMay 2026Exports +53.2% YoY to a record $87.75B; semiconductor exports alone +169.4% to $37.16B, driven by HBM/DRAM pricing.
macroYTD May 2026KOSPI up ~90-100% YTD, led by Samsung and SK Hynix — among the best-performing major indices globally.
macroMay 2026Bank of Korea and KDI both raise 2026 GDP growth forecasts (to 2.5-2.6%) almost entirely on the chip supercycle.
macro2026"One-legged growth": strip out semiconductors and export growth collapses to ~1.7%; autos, refining and steel all struggling.
macro2026Youth employment falling for 24 straight months — chip boom's job-creation multiplier is low, growth benefits stay concentrated.
