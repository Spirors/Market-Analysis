AI market sentiment gauge — bull vs bear, January–July 2026

Every event carries an editable, signed weight from −10 (maximally bearish) to +10 (maximally bullish). 0 means genuinely neutral or too contested to call — the middle column holds events like circular financing where the bull and bear cases are both live arguments, not a hedge. Drag the slider or type a number and the gauge above recalculates live. Defaults are directional judgment calls, skewed heavier for more recent events since they're fresher inputs to current pricing. Micro = company/industry-specific, macro = economy-wide or geopolitical.

Sentiment position = 50 + 50 × (Σ weight) / (Σ |weight|), so it reflects relative balance of conviction, not absolute magnitude.
