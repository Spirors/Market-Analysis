Claude's timing overlay — real dates, not a forecast

Everything below is my probability-weighted read, built from current analyst notes, prediction-market pricing, and scheduled events as of early August 2026 — not investment advice, and not a prediction of what will actually happen. Weigh it against your own thesis above, not instead of it.

~Oct 2026
Anthropic targets an IPO — reportedly racing to list before OpenAI, off a $965B private valuation. First real public-market pricing test for AI valuations.
Nov 3, 2026
US midterms — data-center backlash is already bipartisan: 300+ local moratoria, New York's statewide freeze, ads cutting both ways. Doesn't look likely to resolve cleanly either direction; more likely hardens into permitting friction regardless of who wins.
Sep 16 / Oct 28 / Dec 8–9, 2026
Remaining 2026 FOMC meetings — each is a volatility window given the whiplash between May's hot CPI, June's hawkish hold with a hike pencilled in, and June's sharp CPI cooldown.
By Mar–Jun 2027
OpenAI's own IPO decision point — prediction markets price ~59% odds of an announcement by March 2027, ~73% by June. A more binary test than Anthropic's, given OpenAI's heavier reported losses.
2027
CRE / private-credit maturity wall widely cited as cresting — estimates range roughly $550B–$1.26T maturing that year, landing on the same debt structures (GPU-backed loans, data-center CMBS) already flagged in Phase 1 and Phase 7.
Mid-2027
Analyst-flagged point where OpenAI may need fresh capital without a market debut by then — a dated pressure point, not a certainty.
On Phases 6–7 (trigger + debt crisis): most professional forecasters cluster their highest-risk window at 2027–2028, not 2026 — and most, including the IMF, explicitly expect a gradual differentiation/correction rather than a single 2008-style systemic event. Prediction markets currently price only around 17% odds of a declared "AI bubble burst" by the end of 2026.
On Phase 9 (reflation): the gold leg may already be running ahead of any AI-specific crisis, not after it. Gold hit an all-time high in January 2026 and central banks kept buying at a record pace straight through 2026's price swings — that reads more like a structural de-dollarization/geopolitical story than a "money-printing aftermath" one. Worth treating it as a leading indicator already in motion, not something that only starts once Phase 8 happens.

This is a structured visualization of a stated personal thesis, not a forecast or investment advice — sequencing, magnitude, and timing of Act 2 in particular are genuinely unknown, and Path A vs Path B in Phase 7 is a live fork, not a resolved call.
